Deterministic Access Control Decision Logic

DETERMINISTIC LOGIC

Core Thesis: "Nobody passes without complete protective equipment." The system enforces safety at the physical barrier through a deterministic 4-stage pipeline:

  1. Ultrasonic Detection: HC-SR04 measures target distance continuously. When a worker approaches within 100 cm, Arduino sends D:<cm> to the laptop via serial to initiate verification.
  2. Computer Vision Evaluation: Laptop webcam captures live video. YOLOv8 processes 640×640 frames at 25+ FPS. For access to be granted, both HELMET and VEST must be detected concurrently with confidence ≥ 0.35 across 2 consecutive frames within a 1.2-second window.
  3. Access Granted Outcome: Laptop sends G to Arduino. Green LED activates, Red LED/Buzzer stay OFF, and the SG90 servo sweeps smoothly (1.10s) from 0° to 90°. It rests stationary for 15.0 seconds to allow safe worker entry, then closes (1.10s).
  4. Access Denied Outcome: If either gear item is missing when the verification window expires, laptop sends R to Arduino. Red LED flashes, 2.4kHz buzzer warble sounds, high-resolution snapshot is saved to disk, event logged to CSV, and an instant photo alert is dispatched to Telegram. Barrier remains locked at 0°.
